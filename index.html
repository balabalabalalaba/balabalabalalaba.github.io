01ResearchMain quests

Ultracold quantum gases

2026 - NOWACTIVE QUESTUniversity of Otago, ultracold-atom group of Niels Kjærgaard
  • Feshbach-resonance and atom-loss spectroscopy in an 87Rb Bose–Einstein condensate apparatus, using RF and magnetic-field tuning.
  • Analysis of two-body versus three-body loss and dipolar spin relaxation.
  • Building a 405-to-810 nm type-II BBO SPDC heralded single-photon source to validate g(2) measurements.

Rydberg-atom sensing and many-body physics

2021 - 2026COMPLETEPhD, University of Science and Technology of China
  • Microwave electrometry in thermal Rydberg vapour through heterodyne detection of the microwave-induced optical phase shift.
  • Continuous microwave detection enhanced by stochastic resonance, exploiting Rydberg optical bistability.
  • Chiral switching of many-body steady states around a Liouvillian exceptional point in a dissipative Rydberg gas.
  • Rydberg-atom receivers for digital communication (on-off keying, dual-frequency reception), with communications-engineering collaborators.
  • Microwave-cavity design and EM simulation for atom-cavity strong coupling, and quantum-Fisher-information bounds for field sensing.

Photonic bound states in the continuum

2019 - 2021COMPLETEUndergraduate research, Chongqing University
  • Worked with Prof. Dezhuan Han on the origin of bound states in the continuum (BICs).
  • Wrote a Python package that searches for BICs in 1D photonic-crystal slabs.
